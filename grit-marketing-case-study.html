Grit Marketing Data Platform

A case study of how PeyAI built a reliable ETL platform to centralize ad and lead data, enforce medallion data quality layers, and power repeatable marketing reporting.

Back to Projects

Challenge

Marketing performance and lead data were spread across multiple systems, each with different API formats and extraction constraints. Teams needed one trusted pipeline to ingest daily data, standardize schemas, and prepare analytics-ready datasets without manual spreadsheet work or inconsistent business logic.

Approach

  • Implemented source-specific extractors for Google Ads, Facebook Ads, LeadsHook, and LeadsPedia.
  • Built CLI-driven extraction and transformation jobs with configurable date windows and ingestion options.
  • Applied a medallion architecture (bronze, silver, gold) to separate raw, cleaned, and aggregated layers.
  • Used DuckDB for fast in-pipeline processing and PostgreSQL load integration for durable gold outputs.
  • Added test coverage around transformation and loading logic to improve release confidence.

Outcome

The platform established a repeatable, auditable data workflow from source APIs to analytics-ready tables. This reduced manual reconciliation effort, improved data consistency across reporting views, and created a scalable base for future dashboards and decision support.
